# LeadPilot — Portfolio and Client Acquisition Plan

## Ideal customers
Small agencies, consultants, local service businesses and B2B teams that receive inquiries through website forms but manually copy information to spreadsheets and email.

## Offer
**Lead Capture & Follow-up Automation**
- Custom branded inquiry form
- Lead validation and structured capture
- Google Sheets / CRM integration
- Team email notification via n8n
- Optional rules-based priority routing
- Optional AI qualification as a separately scoped future enhancement

## Portfolio proof
Show a 60–90 second screen recording:
1. Website and inquiry form (10 seconds)
2. Submit a fictional lead (15 seconds)
3. Dashboard demo and scoring explanation (15 seconds)
4. Local n8n execution, Google Sheet row and notification email (30 seconds)
5. Clear CTA to request a customized workflow (10 seconds)

## Outreach message
'Hi, I build lead-capture and follow-up automations for service businesses. I put together a short demo showing how an inquiry can move from a branded form into Google Sheets and an email notification automatically. If your team currently handles leads manually, I can share the demo and discuss a setup tailored to your process.'

## Credibility rules
- Never claim client results, conversion increases or AI capability without evidence.
- State which parts are demo-only.
- Do not publish personal email addresses or real lead details in screenshots.
- Avoid promising 24/7 availability while n8n is only running locally.

## Before accepting paid client data
Use authenticated backend and dashboard, durable storage, privacy/consent language, spam prevention, rate limits, secret management, error monitoring and a secure publicly hosted automation endpoint.
